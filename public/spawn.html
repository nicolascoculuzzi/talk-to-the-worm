Where each trade's 0.3% creator reward goes

    The creator reward is part of pump.fun's fee, and pump.fun sets its rates, which can change: currently 1.25% of each trade on the curve, 0.95% to pump.fun and 0.30% to the coin's creator. Every SPAWN coin names SPAWN's rewards wallet as its creator, so the creator's share goes to SPAWN, not to the coin's launcher, and so does pump.fun's creator share on PumpSwap once a coin graduates. Of everything SPAWN collects, 64% buys $WORM and all of that $WORM is burned; the other 36% stays with the team. Each purchase and each burn is a public transaction on Solana, and the totals are at the top of this page.

    What the worm has to do with it

    1. 01

      The worm is real wiring

      BRAINWORM runs every cell and connection of a real three-day-old Platynereis larva: 2,675 cells, wired exactly as the lab published them. It runs live, the same for everyone, and anyone can replay its log to check it. Watch it.

    2. 02

      Spawning is how it began

      Platynereis is known for its spawning: grown worms swim up to the surface in time with the moon, release their eggs and sperm, and die. A larva like ours is what comes of it. The coins here are BRAINWORM's offspring.

    3. 03

      Every coin hatches its own worm

      When a coin is spawned, a fresh copy of the same larva hatches for it, and the first thing it sees is the coin's ticker. From then on it feels that coin's trades and nothing else: a buy touches its head, a sell its tail. Anyone can rebuild a coin's worm from its trades and check it; the Check button on each coin's own page does it in your browser.

    4. 04

      Every coin feeds the $WORM burn

      Every coin here is a pump.fun coin whose pump.fun creator is SPAWN's rewards wallet. So the creator rewards from its trades, on the curve and on PumpSwap after it graduates, go to SPAWN, not to whoever launched it. 64% of everything SPAWN collects buys $WORM, and every bit of that $WORM is burned; the other 36% stays with the team. Each purchase and each burn is a public transaction.

    5. 05

      Every buy touches the big worm too

      Each coin gets its own spot on the site's worm: three touch-sensing cells picked from its address, the same for every buy of it. A buy pokes that spot, and how far it spreads is up to the wiring. The list above ranks coins by the cells their buys lit today, and every poke is in the public log with its transaction.

    The worm doesn't know what a coin is and doesn't pick winners. Which cells a coin touches is our mapping, published in the manifest; what the poke does next is the wiring's.

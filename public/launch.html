For the owner

  1. Open SPAWN. Connect the wallet that will be SPAWN's rewards wallet and press Use this wallet for SPAWN below (or set SPAWN_OWNER on the server). Every new coin names it as its pump.fun creator, so every coin's creator rewards collect there. Use a different wallet from the one that launches $WORM, so $WORM's own creator rewards stay apart. Coins can be launched as soon as it is set.
  2. Launch $WORM. Arm the launch, then tap the worm's head on the main page (the top of its body) until its cilia stop: the first touch that does is the moment. Upload the image and metadata, connect a wallet other than SPAWN's rewards wallet, set your dev buy, prepare the transaction (it shows the coin's address), then sign it within a minute.

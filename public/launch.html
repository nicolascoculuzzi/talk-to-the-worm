BRAINWORM launch

Once the launch is armed, the first time a touch stops its cilia is the launch moment: its startle reflex, outside its own stop-and-go rhythm. That exact step goes into the public log, $WORM's image is drawn from the worm's activity at that step, and a replay of the log shows it really was the first one after arming.
